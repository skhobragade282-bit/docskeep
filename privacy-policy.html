Privacy Policy

DocsKeep · Last updated September 15, 2026

Your documents

DocsKeep stores your document content on your device. When you enable DocsKeep Pro's iCloud Sync & Backup, your documents are stored in your own private iCloud account. Your document files and any text extracted from them are never sent to a DocsKeep server — we do not operate servers that receive your document content.

Camera access

DocsKeep requests camera access only to let you scan documents you choose to save. Scanned images are processed and stored on your device (and in your iCloud, if enabled); they are not transmitted anywhere else.

Purchases

DocsKeep Pro purchases and subscription billing are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. DocsKeep receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock Pro features — we do not receive or store your payment details.

Your choices

You can delete documents from DocsKeep at any time. Deleting a document removes it and its reminders locally, and syncs that removal to your other devices when iCloud Sync is enabled with Pro. Backups and earlier sync revisions remain in your iCloud Drive until you remove them yourself. Turning off iCloud Sync, or letting Pro expire, pauses app-initiated sync and backups; your local documents remain fully available on-device.

Data sharing

DocsKeep does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your document data or any other information to anyone.

Children's privacy

DocsKeep is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
