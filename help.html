Help & How to Use DocsKeep

Last updated September 15, 2026

Adding a document

Tap the add button on the Home tab, then choose how you'd like to add it:

  • Scan PRO — capture one or more pages with your camera and save them as a single PDF.
  • Photos — import an existing photo of a document from your library.
  • Files — import a PDF or image already saved on your device or in iCloud Drive.

After adding, you can crop, adjust, and review the document before saving it, along with its name, category, and expiry date.

Organizing your documents

Every document belongs to a category, shown with an icon or emoji. DocsKeep includes a set of default categories to get you started. With DocsKeep Pro, you can create your own custom categories with a name and icon of your choice.

Use the Records tab to browse and filter all your saved documents, and the History tab to see recent activity.

Expiry reminders

Give a document an expiry date and DocsKeep can remind you before it lapses:

  • Free plan: one reminder per document, chosen from 30, 7, or 1 day before expiry.
  • DocsKeep Pro PRO: add multiple reminders per document, and choose any number of days from 1 to 365 before expiry.

Reminders are delivered as notifications, so make sure notifications are allowed for DocsKeep in iOS Settings. Open the app occasionally to keep reminders scheduled — DocsKeep keeps your nearest upcoming alerts refreshed automatically.

iCloud Sync & Backup PRO

Turn on iCloud Sync & Backup in Account to keep your documents up to date across your devices and store recovery backups in your own private iCloud storage.

  • Tap Sync Now any time to sync immediately.
  • Tap Find iCloud Backups to see and restore from earlier backups — restoring only adds back documents that are currently missing, without changing what you already have.
  • If Pro expires, sync and backup pause, but all documents already on your device remain fully available.

Managing your subscription

DocsKeep Pro is billed through your Apple Account. To view, change, or cancel your subscription, go to Account → Manage Subscription in the app, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you've reinstalled DocsKeep or switched devices, use Restore Purchases in Account or on the upgrade screen.
